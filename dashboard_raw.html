Drive Endurance

Think of this like a car's tire tread. Every time we write data, a tiny bit of the "tread" wears down. 98.4% means the drive is still very healthy.

PE Cycles

Program/Erase Cycles. One cycle is like writing on a piece of paper and then erasing it. SSDs can only do this a certain number of times (e.g., 10,000) before the paper tears.

Write Amplification (WAF)

This measures how much extra work the drive does behind the scenes. A WAF of 1.0 is perfect (1 unit of user data = 1 unit written). Higher numbers mean more wear.

Garbage Collection (GC)

Just like cleaning a room to find more space, the drive cleans up deleted data to make room for new files.
